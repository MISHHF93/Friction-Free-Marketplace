# Store review notes

Friction-Free Marketplace connects buyers and sellers of physical goods. Purchases use Stripe Connect; the application does not unlock digital content or features through external payment.

Reviewers can browse without an account. Provide temporary buyer and seller credentials in the private review fields. The seller account should contain a test listing; the buyer account should be able to message, make an offer, and complete a test-mode checkout in the review environment.

Native functionality includes universal/app links, camera and photo selection for listings, operating-system sharing, Android back navigation, safe-area/status-bar integration, and connection recovery. Account deletion is available under Account settings and does not require contacting support.

Support: `https://<production-host>/contact`

Privacy: `https://<production-host>/privacy`

Demo notes, final company contact details, regional availability, and any test payment instructions must be completed in App Store Connect and Play Console before submission.
